You're staring at your Shippo dashboard, looking at a list of shipments. You know the real story, the 'why' behind your rising shipping costs and delayed deliveries, is somewhere in that data. Shippo's reporting covers the label, the spend, and the carrier scan; what happens after the scan, from notification engagement to support-ticket volume to on-time performance against the date you promised the shopper, is where you hit the reporting ceiling.

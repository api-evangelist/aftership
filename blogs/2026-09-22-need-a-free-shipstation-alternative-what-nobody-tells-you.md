ShipStation's cheapest plan is $14.99 a month for up to 50 shipments, which is why new stores go looking for a free alternative. The catch: the "free" tools each charge you somewhere else: a monthly label cap, a two-carrier limit, or an automatic jump to a paid plan the moment you grow. AfterShip Shipping's free plan covers 10 labels a month across 100+ carriers, and the first paid step is $11 a month for up to 100 labels, so you start free and scale on a straight line instead of hitting a wall.

If you ship 1,000 to 10,000 orders a month from your own stock, Shippo and ShipHero answer different questions. Shippo is shipping software for the team at the packing bench; ShipHero is warehouse management software for running a warehouse, and it no longer offers outsourced fulfillment. For a packing-room team the choice is shipping software, and the bigger gain comes after the label, where AfterShip competes.

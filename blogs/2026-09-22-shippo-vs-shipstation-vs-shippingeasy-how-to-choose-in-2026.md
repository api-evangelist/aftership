You've narrowed your shipping software to Shippo, ShipStation and ShippingEasy. All three print discounted labels and email your shoppers tracking updates. What none of them documents is an alert to your shopper when a parcel stalls or a delivery fails, and that is where "where is my order?" tickets come from.

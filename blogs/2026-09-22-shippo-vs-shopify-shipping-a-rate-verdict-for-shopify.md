You are looking at your Shopify shipping costs and wondering whether Shippo would make them smaller. On the parcels most small stores send, the published rates are close, and Shopify's own number is visible only inside your admin. The difference that lasts is which carrier service gets chosen for each order, and what happens to that order after the label prints.

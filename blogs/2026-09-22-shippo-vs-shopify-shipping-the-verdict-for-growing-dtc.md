Congratulations on 1,000 orders a month. Before you replace Shopify Shipping, check what it and Shopify Flow already do, because several of the usual reasons to leave no longer apply. The reasons that remain are regional carriers, a proper rules screen and, above all, what happens after the label: which carrier delivers on each lane, and what your customer sees while they wait.

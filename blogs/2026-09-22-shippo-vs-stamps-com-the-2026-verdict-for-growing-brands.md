Your order volume just doubled, and printing labels is now your biggest bottleneck. Shippo and Stamps.com both fix the label: both give you USPS Commercial pricing, and both email your customer a tracking link. Neither publishes a self-service returns portal, and neither web app shows your customer a delivery date before they buy.

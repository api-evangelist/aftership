At 25 orders a day, copying addresses into a label tool stops being a chore and becomes the bottleneck. Stamps.com and Shippo both import Shopify orders and print labels in batches, so the real difference is how each is built and priced for an online store. Past the label, the tracking emails, returns and delivery exceptions decide how many "where is my order?" messages you answer.

Your CFO will not sign off on a visibility platform because the demo looked good. The sticker price is rarely the true cost, and the number that decides the deal is supply chain visibility software TCO: everything the platform costs across the contract, set against everything it returns. Freight-visibility incumbents publish no price at all, which leaves you building that model one sales call at a time.

Past ten clients, a third-party logistics (3PL) operation's shipping platform stops being a back-office tool and becomes part of the service it sells. Both ShipStation and AfterShip run many clients side by side, so client count alone does not decide which platform a growing 3PL outgrows. What decides the next five years is what each client's customers see after the label prints, and whether you can sell that experience back to your clients.

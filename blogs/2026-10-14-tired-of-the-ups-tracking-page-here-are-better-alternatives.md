Why the Default UPS Tracking Page Is Failing Your Brand Your brand is nowhere in sight, the status reads 'In Transit: Departed from Facility,' and the only next step the page offers is a UPS account - not a way back to you. Your customer clicked "Track My Order" in your shipping email and landed on ups.com. For a Shopify brand shipping 1,000 to 20,000 orders a month, that page turns a simple delivery question into a support ticket and hands the post-purchase moment to the carrier.

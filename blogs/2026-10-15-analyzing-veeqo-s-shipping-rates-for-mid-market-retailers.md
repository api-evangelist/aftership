Your CFO wants shipping costs down, and you have been asked to find the platform that gets them there. At 15,000 orders a month, the software line is rarely what moves the total. What moves it is which carrier each label goes to, how much of that choice runs on rules and data, and whether a regional carrier you rely on is supported.

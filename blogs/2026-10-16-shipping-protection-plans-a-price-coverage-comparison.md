Your shipping-issues line item is growing, and the protection plan you pick decides whether it shrinks or just moves. Judge plans on three things: what the shopper pays, what the fine print excludes, and who owns the claim. For a US brand shipping more than 5,000 orders a year, a plan where the claim starts on your own branded tracking page and the insurer reimburses you is the stronger choice.

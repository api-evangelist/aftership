Your sales are growing, and printing shipping labels has quietly become an unpaid job. If you have narrowed the search to ShippingEasy vs Shippo, both will fix that part: both connect to your store, pull orders in, and print discounted labels without you retyping an address. The choice that lasts is a different question, and it turns on where you sell and what the tool still connects to as your order count climbs.

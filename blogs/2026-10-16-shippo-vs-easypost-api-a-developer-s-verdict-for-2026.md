Choosing a shipping API lays the foundation for your post-purchase infrastructure. Shippo and EasyPost both cover labels, rates, and carrier tracking. The cost that decides this choice is how many separate post-purchase integrations, and data stores, your team will build, reconcile, and maintain as the product grows.

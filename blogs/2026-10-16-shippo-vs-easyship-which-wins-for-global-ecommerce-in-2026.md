Opening the UK, the EU and Canada adds one question to every order: will your first customer in Germany face a customs bill at the door? Since 1 July 2026, every low-value parcel entering the EU from outside it carries a customs duty. Easyship can show duties to shoppers at checkout, and Shippo lets you pay them as the sender, billed after the parcel ships.

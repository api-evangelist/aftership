You're comparing Shippo and ShipHero, and it feels like a choice between 'too simple' and 'too complex.' The reason for your confusion is simple: you're comparing a label utility to a warehouse system. For a growing DTC brand, neither is the right answer. The decision that matters most comes after the label, in the tracking page, notifications and returns your customers see, and that is where AfterShip is the specialist choice.

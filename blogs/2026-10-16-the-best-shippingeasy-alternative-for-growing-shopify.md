If every order that matches a rule still goes to the carrier you picked months ago, whatever that carrier's delivery record on your lanes looks like now, you have outgrown your shipping setup. Both ShippingEasy and AfterShip automate order handling with if/then rules. AfterShip Shipping also re-evaluates each shipment's carrier on live rates and lane-level delivery data, and AfterShip Tracking and AfterShip Returns tell shoppers about delays and let them start returns themselves.
